Sessions

Sessions are usually saved in C:\Users\<username>\.codex\sessions on Windows, or ~/.codex/sessions on macOS / Linux.

Select the sessions folder itself. Sessions in its dated subfolders will appear below. If you changed CODEX_HOME, look in its sessions folder.

Titles use the first question. For saved names, also open .codex/session_index.jsonl with Open files.
